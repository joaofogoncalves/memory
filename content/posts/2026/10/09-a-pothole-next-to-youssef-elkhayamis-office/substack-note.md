A pothole next to Youssef Elkhayami's office in the Netherlands had survived years of complaints, so he took six photos, gave them to Claude, had it rebuild the hole in 3D, accurate to the millimeter, and asked what happens when a car hits it at 30 km/h. https://x.com/yelkhayami/status/2108227066621465007

What came back was a liability simulator in which the tire drops in, the rim folds, the car behind brakes too late, a bakfiets swerves into a delivery scooter and the pizza lands on a Tesla, at an estimated €21,340 in damage per incident and four incidents a month.

He sent the gemeente, the local city council, the slow-motion version, with sound, and a polite note asking who they would like the invoices addressed to, and he says a road crew filled it two days later.
