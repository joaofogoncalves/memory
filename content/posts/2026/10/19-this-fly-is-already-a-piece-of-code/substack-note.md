This fly is already a piece of code living in the matrix, and judging by the footage it is having a lovely time.

Over the weekend Odyssey placed a simulated fruit fly brain inside Odyssey-3, its frontier world model, and the clip follows it along a sunny harbour that exists only because the model generates it. In the replies Oliver Cameron explained the world is just a prompt, which Odyssey-3 then simulates in real time. https://x.com/odysseyml/status/2111589148146749616

The best-known fly brain model is built from the FlyWire wiring map of the adult fly, about 140,000 neurons and 50 million connections turned into a network that reacts the way its wiring says it should. In March Eon Systems connected it to a virtual body in a hand-built physics simulation, and Odyssey has now given a fly brain a world learned from video. https://eon.systems/updates/embodied-brain-emulation

Odyssey posted the clip without a methods write-up, so which brain model sits inside and how it connects are theirs to share. World models have mostly been shown as training grounds for robots and game agents, and an insect's brain flying around inside one makes it easy to picture these worlds filling up with stranger residents.
