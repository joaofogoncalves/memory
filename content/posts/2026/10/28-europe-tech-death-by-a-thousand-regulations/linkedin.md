# LinkedIn: europe-tech-death-by-a-thousand-regulations

Published as text only (no image). Link-test post 3: sources in the first comment.

---

Europe has around 100 tech laws and more than 270 digital regulators, and Mario Draghi counted them in his own report.

The chart showing Europe's share of tech R&D falling to 8% is real, and the number holds up. I don't think one rule did it. I think it is death by a thousand regulations, plus a few other things that bite just as hard.

Draghi lists them. 27 rulebooks applied 27 different ways. Europe raises 5% of the world's venture capital, the US raises 52%. Of 147 European unicorns founded between 2008 and 2021, 40 moved their headquarters abroad, most of them to the US. Electricity costs 2 to 3 times what it does in the US.

Regulation is a real part of the bill for the smallest companies. After GDPR, weekly EU tech venture deals fell 17.6% against the US, and the youngest startups took the hardest hit. Meta AI took about nine months to reach Europe.

The good news is that Europe is now cutting some of it. The AI Act's hardest rules were pushed back to 2027 and 2028, and last week the Council backed EU Inc., a company you could register online in up to two days for at most 100 euros. Parliament has to move on it by early 2027, and that is the one to watch.
