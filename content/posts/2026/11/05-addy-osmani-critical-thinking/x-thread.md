For every green check, name one thing it didn't test.

I agree with most of it, but it only sticks if the team budgets for it. Addy says it himself: checking the machine properly is real work, so schedule it as a line item. If you measure PRs merged and tasks closed, that minute is the first one to go.

Slow the agents down before you skip the review. Agents make the checks cheap to build, and deciding what counts as verified stays human.
