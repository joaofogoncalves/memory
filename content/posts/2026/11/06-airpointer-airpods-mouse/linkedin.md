About 1.5 million views later, a closed Apple AirPods case is moving a cursor on a MacBook.

Andy Gao recorded himself pointing the case at the screen, scrolling, and clicking. Someone asked why, and he said he wanted to see if it was possible. He also said it could be useful for presentations and games, like a Wii remote.

This morning he published the code as AirPointer. You move the closed case to move the cursor and tap the front to click. He has tested it on one Mac with AirPods Pro 3.

I like the presentation version. A lot of clickers are one more thing to charge and remember. This one is the case already in your pocket when you walk up to talk.
