About 1.5 million views later, a closed AirPods case is moving a cursor on a MacBook.

Someone asked @instantricecook why he built it. Andy Gao said he wanted to see if it was possible, and that it could work for presentations and games, like a Wii remote.

This morning he published the code as AirPointer. You move the closed case to move the cursor and tap the front to click.

The version I would actually use is a clicker for talks. The case is already in your pocket when you walk up to present.
