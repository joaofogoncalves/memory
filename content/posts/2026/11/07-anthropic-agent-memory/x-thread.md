Claude Fable 5 kept nine memory files plus a short dashboard while playing Pokémon and earned four badges. Sonnet 3.7 wrote 413 files across 225 folders and earned one.

Around step 10,000, Sonnet 3.7 spent 17 steps creating and deleting notes and took no actions in the game.

A longer context window still belongs to one session. Memory is what the next session can read. @AnthropicAI's engineering post calls the cleanup step dreaming: idle time used to reread logs, correct notes, and organize what to load next time.

They borrow the word from how brains replay experience during sleep. The cleanup that matters is checking those notes against the original transcript before the next session trusts them.
