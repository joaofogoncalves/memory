1,925 solar boxes with a robot arm inside are doing the beekeeping now.

Cameras spot Varroa mites without opening the hive, and the arm heats the brood until the mites die and the bees don't. No chemicals.

Hive sensors existed for years and mostly went unused. The version that acts is the one that scaled.
