@Anthropic's new Forward Deployed Engineer path has 11 courses and about 9.5 hours of material, and it ends with a single document: a pilot plan for one customer use case.

That choice says a lot about the job. The plan asks for the baseline you will measure against, the success measures you agree on before building, the first part of the use case you ship, and how you bring on the first wave of users. Most of the hours go to the tools, from the agent loop to Claude Code, MCP and skills. The two delivery courses take about 100 minutes, and one of them says plainly that the technical deployment is the easier part.

That matches what I see working with enterprise teams. The code gets written fast now. What takes the time is finding a real baseline, because often nobody has measured the process they want to improve, and then helping people change how they work once the pilot is live.

I'm glad to see that written into a curriculum. Engineers who take this path will start their first engagement knowing a pilot is judged by whether people use it.

The path is open to Claude Partner Network members: https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/path/claude-fde
