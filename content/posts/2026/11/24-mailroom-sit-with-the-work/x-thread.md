A mailroom step that took five minutes per letter became two API calls after a senior developer sat and watched the work.

That scene opens @AnthropicAI's new paper, Working with AI, from 66 interviews with 58 companies.

1. At a midsize law firm, a clerk opened each scanned letter, renamed it, and filed it. Five minutes per letter.

2. A senior developer went to the mailroom and watched the work.

3. In a month it was two API calls: one names the document from its first page, the other files it. The clerks "were blown away."

4. The same developer then built a conflict-check agent in under a week. The process around it was never redesigned. The staff pushed back, and the agent was shelved.

This is where the weeks go: sitting with the people who already do the work, and staying until the new process is running in production.
